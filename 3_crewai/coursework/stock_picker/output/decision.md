Companies not selected and why

Advanced Micro Devices (AMD)
- AMD is a strong growth candidate and has improved meaningfully in AI servers and AI accelerators.
- However, it still trails NVIDIA by a wide margin in software ecosystem, installed base, developer mindshare, and overall market leadership.
- It offers more upside torque, but also more execution risk and less certainty than NVIDIA.
- That makes AMD attractive, but not the best single investment choice.

Micron Technology
- Micron has compelling AI-driven memory demand and could benefit from tight supply and strong pricing.
- The issue is that memory remains inherently cyclical, even if AI is helping stabilize the cycle.
- Micron lacks the same structural moat and ecosystem advantage that NVIDIA has.
- It is a strong thematic investment, but its returns are more dependent on commodity-like pricing and cycle management.

OpenAI
- OpenAI is one of the most important AI platform companies in the world and has exceptional strategic relevance.
- It was not selected because it is not currently publicly listed, so it is not an immediately actionable public-market investment.
- Its future IPO could be significant, but timing, governance, valuation, and monetization uncertainty remain major variables.
- It is a compelling future opportunity, but not the best current investable company from this list.

SpaceX
- SpaceX has outstanding private-market strength and leadership in launch services and satellite internet.
- It was not selected because it is also not publicly listed, limiting direct investment access.
- It has major long-term potential, but it is less straightforward as a current investment option than a public company like NVIDIA.
- Its private-market nature, liquidity constraints, and capital intensity make it less suitable as the best immediate pick.

Final conclusion
- NVIDIA is the best company for investment as of 2026-07-22 because it offers the strongest combination of dominant market position, durable AI demand exposure, and long-term growth potential.
